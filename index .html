V1.7 limitations

V1.7 adds extra automatically generated corridor alternatives, visible route selection, device location, an optional via point, persistent RV profile values, and Google Maps handoff. Place search uses Mapbox first with an OpenStreetMap fallback. Elevation and RV-specific legal restrictions still require independent verification. It calculates curvature from route geometry and estimates grades from sampled SRTM elevation. It still does not know true lane count, shoulder width, propane restrictions, axle limits, or every RV-specific legal restriction. Elevation sampling is a prototype estimate and can exaggerate very short grade changes. Always verify critical restrictions with posted signs and authoritative sources.
