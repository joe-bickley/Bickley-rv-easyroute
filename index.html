V1.5 limitations

V1.5 uses Mapbox first and, when Mapbox cannot confidently resolve a named park or POI, falls back to an interactive OpenStreetMap lookup; weak matches still require confirmation. It calculates curvature from route geometry and estimates grades from sampled SRTM elevation. It still does not know true lane count, shoulder width, propane restrictions, axle limits, or every RV-specific legal restriction. Elevation sampling is a prototype estimate and can exaggerate very short grade changes. Always verify critical restrictions with posted signs and authoritative sources.
